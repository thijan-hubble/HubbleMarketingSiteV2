Cookies are small data files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work or work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

We use different types of cookies for various purposes:

Essential Cookies: Necessary for the Site to function properly (e.g., enabling navigation).
Performance and Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our Site by collecting and reporting information anonymously (e.g., Google Analytics).
Functionality Cookies: Allow the Site to remember choices you make and provide enhanced, more personalized features.
Marketing/Advertising Cookies: Used to deliver advertisements relevant to your interests and measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.
You have the ability to accept or decline cookies. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can usually modify your browser setting to decline cookies if you prefer. However, if you choose to decline cookies, some features of our Site may not function properly.
